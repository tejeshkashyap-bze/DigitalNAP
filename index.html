Beyond Zero Emissions

How ready is Australian industry for a clean economy?

Australia can be a clean energy and green export superpower, but that ambition only counts once it turns into built projects — region by region. Most of our heavy industry sits in a handful of places, and each of them needs the same things to make the shift: renewable power, transmission and ports, supportive policy, skilled people, and the backing of the community around it.

The National Action Plan looks at 16 of those industrial regions. It assesses each one against the same set of measures, scores every measure from 1 to 5, and publishes the evidence behind the score. Assessments have been published for 4 of them so far.
